      Missables & warnings

      Rules that protect your run

      • Manual saves. Save at the start of every chapter and before the final mission. There is no mission replay, so a chapter-start save is the only way back.
      • No cheats on your trophy save. Turning on a cheat disables trophies and saving, according to PowerPyx via GTABase.
      • One playthrough is enough. Every trophy is single-player and offline; nothing in the list needs online play, a difficulty setting, or a second run.
      • Choices count. “Actions Speak Louder” tracks choices you make (PlayStation tracks a count toward 500), so choose actively instead of always taking the default — skipped choices cannot be re-made.
      • Unconfirmed means unconfirmed. A few trophies had no confirmed unlock method at launch (see the honesty tab). Do not grind for them blindly; check a current community guide first.

      Sources & honesty

      What this guide is built on, and what it does not know yet.

        What is verified

        • All 55 names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list as reported 18 Nov 2026 — Exophase and PSNProfiles agree on every entry.
        • 28 of the 55 are hidden on PlayStation until earned; all 55 are single-player, with no online trophies.
        • Counts PlayStation tracks (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone race locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garages) come from the official list data.

        What is not verified yet

        • The game released 19 Nov 2026, so step-by-step methods for a few trophies had no confirmed source at launch: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin, plus Challenging Tank, Personal Best and Actions Speak Louder. Their guides say so plainly instead of inventing a method.
        • Only one trophy is flagged missable by the sources (Positive Mental Attitude); whether any other trophy can be missed is still unconfirmed, so the roadmap tells you to keep chapter-start saves rather than promise that nothing else can be missed.
        • Guide text is written in our own words for this page. It draws on the sources listed above and cites them per trophy; it never copies another guide’s text.
